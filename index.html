About Nour

A Vision Rooted in
Light & Story

I am Nour Hammoud — a photographer based in the heart of Beirut, driven by an unrelenting passion for authentic storytelling. My lens finds beauty in the unscripted: the tear on a father's cheek, the amber light pooling over the Corniche at dusk, the defiant colour of a Gemmayzeh mural.

Formally trained at the Lebanese Academy of Fine Arts (ALBA) and later at the École Nationale Supérieure Louis-Lumière in Paris, I bring a rigorous technical eye to deeply human subjects. Every frame I compose is a letter — intimate, honest, and lasting.

From intimate family portraits to lavish mountain weddings and editorial campaigns, I tailor each session to reflect your unique narrative. Lebanon is my muse; every client, a new chapter.
